# Product principles

1. **Race-day reliability over feature breadth** — losing times is worse than missing a nice-to-have.
2. **Timekeeper speed first** — large targets, undo, minimal typing friction.
3. **Honest current vs target documentation** — never market unimplemented behaviour.
4. **Timing data integrity** — prefer append/correct over silent mutation as the product matures.
5. **Right-sized for clubs** — avoid enterprise complexity and “sports platform” sprawl.
6. **Incremental evolution** — improve the working core; don’t rewrite for sport.
7. **Organisation owns the data** — commercial direction assumes club-owned archives, not only browser profiles.
8. **Single-purpose clarity** — Split Times is about timing and results, not full club administration.
