# Product vision

## Current

A practical single-device browser tool for capturing mass-start split times and time-trial start/finish times, with local history and simple results copy-out.

## Target MVP (commercial)

A reliable **club-oriented race timing product** that a small triathlon/running/cycling club or local organiser can:

1. Own as an organisation account
2. Set up a race with numbered (and optionally named) participants
3. Time confidently on race day on a phone/tablet
4. Keep results safely beyond a single browser profile
5. Export/share results with members

MVP deliberately avoids becoming a full chip-timing or federation results platform.

## Future

- Multi-device timekeeping with conflict-safe timing records
- Stronger offline race-day mode
- Categories and basic rankings
- Coach/training features beyond simple TT
- Public results pages
- Integrations (club membership tools, payment for entries) as needed

## Non-goals (near term)

- Replacing professional chip timing for large mass-participation events
- Full athlete social network
- Generic “sports club OS” (membership, finance, facility booking)
