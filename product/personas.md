# Personas (proposed)

These personas are **Proposed** for product planning. They are not encoded in the app.

## P1 — Sam the club timekeeper

Volunteer who times club handicaps and small races on a phone. Cares about speed, large tap targets, undo, and not losing data if the browser crashes.

## P2 — Riley the race organiser

Organises a local club open-water or multi-stage club race a few times a year. Needs setup beforehand, trustworthy results afterward, and something cheaper/simpler than full chip timing.

## P3 — Alex the club admin

Pays for tools, manages who can access club data, worries about GDPR and “what if the tablet is wiped”.

## P4 — Chris the coach

Runs time trials on midweek sessions. Wants minimal setup and a list of times to paste into a spreadsheet or chat.

## P5 — Jordan the athlete (future)

Wants to see results on their phone after the race without finding the timekeeper.
